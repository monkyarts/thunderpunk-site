Privacy Policy – ThunderPunk Uploader

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ThunderPunk Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the ThunderPunk YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account authorization tokens, granted by the channel owner through Google OAuth.
  • Basic YouTube channel information (channel name and ID), used only to confirm the upload goes to the correct channel.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and captions that the owner chooses to upload.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other Google users.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation system and are never shown publicly. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
