ThunderPunk Uploader

ThunderPunk Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel ThunderPunk (@yothunderpunk), a 2D cartoon comedy channel.

What the app does

The app uploads finished, owner-approved animated videos to the ThunderPunk YouTube channel, and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and privacy setting. It uses the YouTube Data API with the permissions "Upload YouTube videos" and "Manage your YouTube account".

The app is not offered to the public. It acts only on the channel owner's own account, with the owner's permission.
